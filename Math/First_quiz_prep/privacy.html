Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This page explains what information the MAT 101 Quiz Preparation site handles and how it is used. The site is an educational quiz-preparation project.

1. Information collected

  • Quiz progress: the site may save your current question, selected answers, score-related state and remaining quiz time so that you can continue later.
  • Local browser storage: a copy of quiz progress may be kept in your browser's local storage on the device you use.

2. Why the information is used

Quiz progress is used only to save and restore your preparation activity and to provide the quiz features. The current site does not require an account or a server-side quiz-progress service.

3. Where information is stored

Quiz progress is saved in your browser's local storage on the device you use. It is not uploaded to a user account or synchronised across devices. Local progress may remain in your browser until you clear the site's stored data.

4. Sharing and advertising

This site is not designed to sell personal information or use quiz data for advertising. Quiz answers and progress remain in the browser's local storage; the site does not send them to a server or synchronise them across devices. The website and its static resources are hosted through GitHub Pages and GitHub-hosted files.

5. Data retention and deletion

Saved progress may remain in your browser until it is cleared. You can clear local site data through your browser settings.

6. Security

The site relies on your browser's local storage for saved progress. Anyone with access to your browser profile may be able to access that locally saved progress. No online service or local storage system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the site develops, especially when new features or data types are introduced. The date above will be changed when a material update is made.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the site owner using the contact number provided on the home page.
