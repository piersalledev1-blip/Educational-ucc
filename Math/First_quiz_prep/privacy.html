Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This page explains what information the MAT101 Quiz Preparation site handles and how it is used. The site is an educational quiz-preparation project.

1. Information collected

  • Account information: if you create an account, your email address and authentication details are handled by Supabase Auth. The site does not receive or store your plain-text password.
  • Quiz progress: the site may save your current question, selected answers, score-related state and remaining quiz time so that you can continue later.
  • Local browser storage: a copy of quiz progress may be kept in your browser's local storage on the device you use.

2. Why the information is used

Account information is used to sign you in. Quiz progress is used only to save and restore your preparation activity and to provide the quiz features. The countdown uses the server's current time and a published quiz date; it does not need your personal information.

3. Where information is stored

When you are signed in, quiz progress is stored using Supabase, the project's database and authentication provider. Access rules are intended to ensure that a signed-in user can access only that user's saved quiz progress. Local progress may also remain in your browser until you clear the site's stored data.

4. Sharing and advertising

This site is not designed to sell personal information or use quiz data for advertising. The site loads the Supabase JavaScript library from a content-delivery network and communicates with Supabase to provide account and saving features. Those providers may process technical connection information as part of delivering their services; their own privacy terms also apply.

5. Data retention and deletion

Saved progress may remain in your browser or account until it is cleared or deleted. You can clear local site data through your browser settings. To request deletion of account-related data stored by the project, contact the site owner/administrator. A self-service account deletion control is not currently provided.

6. Security

The project uses Supabase authentication and database access controls. Do not reuse a password that you use for another important account. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the site develops, especially when new features or data types are introduced. The date above will be changed when a material update is made.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the site owner through the contact channel provided by the course/site administrator. The project owner should add a direct contact address here before the site is shared widely.
